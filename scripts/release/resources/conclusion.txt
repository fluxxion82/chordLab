ChordLab is installed.

Ableton Live: rescan plug-ins (Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan) or restart Live, then find ChordLab under Plug-Ins > VST3. Put it on a MIDI track and route a second track's MIDI From to "ChordLab" (the plug-in's output), with Monitor set to In. The user guide has the full setup.

Logic Pro: on a software-instrument track, open the MIDI FX slot and choose Audio Units > SterlingAlbury > ChordLab. If it is missing, open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager and click Full Audio Unit Reset, then quit and reopen Logic.

The app is in /Applications/ChordLab.app.

To uninstall, remove:
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ChordLab.vst3
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ChordLab.component
  /Applications/ChordLab.app
